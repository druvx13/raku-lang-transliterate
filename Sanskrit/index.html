ℹ️ About IAST (International Alphabet of Sanskrit Transliteration)

  • Macrons for long vowels: ā, ī, ū
  • Dot-under for retroflex consonants: ṭ, ḍ, ṇ, ṣ
  • Dot-above for anusvara: ṁ
  • Underdot for vocalic r/l: ṛ, ṝ, ḷ, ḹ
  • Tilde for palatal nasal: ñ
  • Acute accent for palatal sibilant: ś
  • Visarga: ḥ
